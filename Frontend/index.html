      ABOUT

      Check a claim before sharing it.

      Fake News Detector gives you a quick first check on suspicious claims and articles, and a starting point for deeper verification.

      Our mission

      Make misinformation easier to spot by giving everyone a simple first check for claims and news articles.

      How it works

      Your text is sent to a machine learning model that compares its writing patterns with the training data and returns a likely real, likely fake, or uncertain label.

      Limitations

      The model does not verify facts. Always cross-check important claims with trusted publishers and official sources before sharing.

      Your data

      Investigation history is stored in the local SQLite database and may be visible to other users of this server. This app does not currently provide user accounts or per-user access controls.
